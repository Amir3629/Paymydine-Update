Printer & cash drawer

One-time setup for this Cashier computer.

Print output

Use a real receipt printer at the restaurant, or Virtual PDF when testing without hardware.

No physical printer is used. Receipt and Invoice jobs are rendered by the same authenticated Electron print window, saved as PDF in Desktop / PayMyDine Print Tests, and opened automatically. Cash drawer commands are disabled in this mode.
Print receipt automatically after payment
Every successful Cashier payment prints once. In Virtual PDF mode, it creates one PDF instead.

Cash drawer

PayMyDine sends an ESC/POS kick through the selected receipt printer. Virtual PDF mode never touches drawer hardware.

Open automatically after cash payment
Only successful Cash payments trigger this in Physical printer mode. Card payments never open the drawer.
